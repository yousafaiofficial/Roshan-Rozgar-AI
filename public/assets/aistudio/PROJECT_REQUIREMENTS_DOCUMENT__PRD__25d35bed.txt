============================================================ 1.
EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
============================================================

Roshan Rozgar is an AI-powered, hyper-local web marketplace designed to
connect households with nearby skilled trade workers, including
electricians, plumbers, AC technicians, carpenters, mechanics and other
service professionals.

A significant proportion of Pakistan’s workforce operates in the
informal economy. Many skilled workers have extensive practical
experience but lack access to conventional digital employment platforms
because these platforms depend on written profiles, CVs, complicated
registration procedures and English-language interfaces.

At the same time, households face difficulties finding reliable,
available and reasonably priced service professionals within their
neighbourhoods.

Roshan Rozgar addresses these challenges through a mobile-first web
application that uses artificial intelligence, voice interaction and
location-based discovery.

Workers can create profiles through spoken Urdu or Roman Urdu instead of
conventional registration forms. Customers can search for nearby
professionals, communicate through voice messages, negotiate prices,
confirm bookings and submit reviews.

The platform aims to make digital employment opportunities more
accessible while improving transparency, convenience and trust in local
service transactions.

============================================================ 2. PROBLEM
STATEMENT ============================================================

Pakistan’s informal workforce includes millions of skilled individuals
who provide essential services to households and businesses.

Traditional employment platforms often fail to accommodate these workers
because they require: - Reading and writing proficiency. -
English-language communication. - Conventional registration forms. - CVs
and formal employment histories. - Technical knowledge of digital
applications.

Consequently, skilled tradespeople frequently depend on personal
contacts, local gathering points (addas), contractors and word-of-mouth
recommendations.

Customers experience a different but related problem. They often lack
reliable information about: - Which workers operate nearby. - Which
workers are currently available. - Whether a worker has relevant
experience. - How much a service might cost. - Whether previous
customers were satisfied.

There is no simple, unified platform addressing both sides of this
problem through an accessible digital experience.

2.1 PROPOSED SOLUTION

Roshan Rozgar will provide a centralized marketplace where: 1. Workers
register through voice-based AI onboarding. 2. Customers discover nearby
workers using service and location filters. 3. Both parties communicate
through voice messages and structured quotations. 4. Customers confirm
bookings through the platform. 5. Completed jobs generate customer
reviews and worker reputation information.

============================================================ 3. PROJECT
OBJECTIVES ============================================================

The primary objectives are: 1. Develop an accessible digital marketplace
for informal skilled workers. 2. Eliminate the need for conventional
written registration through voice-based onboarding. 3. Enable customers
to find nearby and available service professionals. 4. Support
communication in Urdu and Roman Urdu. 5. Simplify job negotiation
through AI-assisted communication. 6. Maintain accurate worker
availability during confirmed bookings. 7. Establish a reputation system
using completed jobs and customer reviews. 8. Demonstrate practical AI
integration that addresses genuine accessibility barriers.

============================================================ 7.
FUNCTIONAL REQUIREMENTS
============================================================

Functional requirements describe what the system must do.

FR-01: USER ROLE SELECTION The system shall provide two primary user
roles: - Worker. - Customer.

Users shall be directed to the appropriate application interface based
on their selected role.

FR-02: VOICE-BASED WORKER ONBOARDING The system shall allow workers to
register without completing a conventional text-based registration form.

The onboarding process shall: 1. Display a prominent voice-recording
control. 2. Capture spoken information. 3. Submit audio to the backend.
4. Convert speech into text. 5. Extract relevant worker information
using an AI language model. 6. Generate a structured profile. 7. Display
extracted information for confirmation. 8. Save the approved profile to
the database.

Expected profile attributes: - Full name. - Trade category. - Years of
experience. - Operating location. - Professional skills. - Short
description. - Starting price. - Availability status.

FR-03: WORKER PROFILE MANAGEMENT The system shall allow workers to: -
View their profiles. - Update profile information. - Modify their
professional description. - Update starting prices. - Change
availability status. - Review customer feedback. - View completed jobs.

FR-04: CUSTOMER DISCOVERY The system shall allow customers to search for
workers using: - Service category. - Location. - Availability. -
Distance. - Experience. - Customer rating.

Search results shall display relevant worker information.

The system shall prioritize suitable workers based on service
compatibility, availability, distance and reputation.

FR-05: WORKER PROFILE VIEWING Customers shall be able to view: - Worker
name. - Profession. - Experience. - Service location. - Starting
price. - Availability. - Customer rating. - Completed jobs. - Previous
customer reviews.

FR-06: VOICE-BASED COMMUNICATION The system shall provide a
communication interface supporting voice messages and text transcripts.

Users shall be able to: - Record voice messages. - Send messages. -
Listen to received audio. - View generated transcripts. - Communicate
job requirements. - Discuss service prices.

FR-07: AI-ASSISTED JOB UNDERSTANDING The AI system shall analyze
customer voice messages and extract: - Requested service. - Problem
description. - Urgency. - Relevant location information, when provided.

The extracted information shall be presented for user confirmation.

FR-08: PRICE NEGOTIATION The system shall support structured price
quotations.

Workers shall be able to: - Submit a proposed price. - Review customer
responses. - Accept a counteroffer. - Submit another quotation.

Customers shall be able to: - View proposed prices. - Accept a
quotation. - Reject a quotation. - Submit a counteroffer.

The final agreed price shall be stored with the booking.

FR-09: BOOKING MANAGEMENT The system shall support the following booking
statuses:

Pending: Request awaiting worker response. Confirmed: Both parties have
agreed. In Progress: Work has started. Completed: Work has finished.
Cancelled: Booking has been cancelled.

The system shall update worker availability when a booking is confirmed.

A worker shall not be assigned overlapping confirmed jobs.

FR-10: WORKER AVAILABILITY The system shall: - Display available
workers. - Display busy workers. - Prevent new confirmed bookings for
engaged workers. - Update availability after job completion. - Update
availability following valid cancellation.

FR-11: CUSTOMER REVIEWS Customers shall be able to submit feedback after
completed jobs.

The review shall include: - Numerical rating from 1 to 5. - Written or
voice feedback. - Optional feedback categories.

Only customers associated with completed bookings shall be permitted to
review the corresponding worker.

FR-12: AI REVIEW ANALYSIS The AI system shall: 1. Transcribe voice
reviews when applicable. 2. Identify relevant feedback. 3. Categorize
feedback into service quality, punctuality and communication. 4.
Generate concise summaries from existing customer reviews.

The system shall not generate unsupported review claims.

FR-13: RESPONSIVE INTERFACE The application shall support: - Desktop
browsers. - Tablet screens. - Smartphone screens.

Worker interfaces shall prioritize large buttons, simple navigation,
readable text and voice-based interaction.

============================================================ 8.
NON-FUNCTIONAL REQUIREMENTS
============================================================

NFR-01: USABILITY The system shall provide a simple interface suitable

NFR-02: ACCESSIBILITY The system shall prioritize: - Large interactive

Restrict access to authorized booking participants. - Prevent
unauthorized review submission. - Protect customer and worker
information.

NFR-05: RELIABILITY The system shall maintain consistency between
booking statuses and worker availability.

NFR-06: MAINTAINABILITY The application shall use modular frontend
components, organized backend routes and clearly structured database
operations.

NFR-07: SCALABILITY The database and application architecture should
support future expansion to additional cities and service categories.

============================================================ 9.
ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE REQUIREMENTS
============================================================

AI is a central component of Roshan Rozgar rather than an optional
decorative feature.

AI-01: SPEECH RECOGNITION Purpose: Convert spoken Urdu, Roman Urdu and
supported languages into text. Input: Audio recordings from workers and
customers. Output: Transcribed text. Technology: OpenAI speech-to-text
API.

AI-02: WORKER INFORMATION EXTRACTION Purpose: Convert natural speech
into structured worker profile information.

Example input: “Mera naam Ali hai. Main electrician hoon aur mujhe 8
saal ka experience hai.”

Expected output: { “name”: “Ali”, “trade”: “Electrician”,
“experience_years”: 8 }

The extracted information shall be validated before database insertion.

AI-03: JOB REQUEST UNDERSTANDING Purpose: Understand customer
requirements from natural speech.

Expected output: { “service”: “AC Repair”, “urgency”: “Today”,
“problem”: “AC not cooling”, “location”: “Bhara Kahu” }

AI-04: AI-ASSISTED NEGOTIATION Purpose: Extract proposed prices and
important job information from voice messages.

Example input: “Main visit ke 500 rupay loon ga.”

Output: { “proposed_price”: 500, “currency”: “PKR” }

The extracted quotation must be confirmed by the worker before being
sent.

AI-05: REVIEW INTELLIGENCE Purpose: Transform voice feedback into
structured and understandable customer feedback.

Expected categories: - Work quality. - Punctuality. - Communication. -
Professionalism.

AI-generated summaries shall be based only on available reviews.

9.1 AI VALIDATION

The system shall account for: - Incorrect speech transcription. -
Ambiguous service descriptions. - Incorrectly extracted prices. -
Unsupported language recognition. - Missing profile information.

Users shall be able to review and correct extracted information before
it is finalized.

============================================================ 16.
BUSINESS RULES
============================================================

The system shall enforce the following rules:

1.  Every worker must have a valid trade category.
2.  Worker experience cannot be negative.
3.  A customer must select a worker before creating a booking.
4.  A booking must initially have a pending status.
5.  A worker must accept a request before it becomes confirmed.
6.  A confirmed booking must contain an agreed price.
7.  A busy worker cannot accept another overlapping confirmed booking.
8.  A completed booking must not be reopened without an authorized
    administrative process.
9.  Customers may review only completed bookings.
10. A booking may receive only one review.
11. A worker’s availability must be restored after valid completion or
    cancellation, provided no other active engagement exists.
12. AI-generated information must be reviewed or confirmed where it
    affects a profile, quotation or booking.

============================================================ 28.
CONCLUSION ============================================================

Roshan Rozgar aims to bridge the gap between Pakistan’s informal skilled
workforce and households that require dependable local services.

Rather than forcing workers to adapt to conventional digital employment
systems, the platform uses voice interaction and artificial intelligence
to make digital participation more accessible.

For customers, it introduces a structured way to discover nearby
workers, compare professional information, negotiate prices, confirm
jobs and evaluate service quality.

The project’s core value is the combination of accessibility, local
discovery, reliable booking and reputation management in one connected
system.
